## Data and services

- **Breached-password check:** Locken checks new passwords against Pwned Passwords by Have I Been Pwned (https://haveibeenpwned.com), licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Only the first 5 characters of a one-way hash of the password are sent.
- **Video titles:** YouTube's oEmbed service (https://www.youtube.com) supplies the title and channel of a video you check.
- **Accounts:** Supabase. **Hosting and updates:** Vercel. **AI features** (Mac, optional, your own key): Anthropic, OpenAI (ChatGPT) and Google Gemini. **Assignments** (Mac, optional): your school's Canvas LMS by Instructure.
