# Step 2, "film": 10 videos, one per complaint

**Made:** October 18, 2026, from the top 10 in `competitor-complaints-2026-10.md`.
**Format:** vertical 9:16, 15–30 seconds, one take where possible. Each video opens on the complaint (the hook), then shows Locken answering it on screen.

## Rules for every video
- **Don't name competitors.** Say "your focus app" or "most blockers". Naming brands invites trademark and false-advertising trouble, and the complaint lands without it.
- **Only show what Locken really does today,** recorded on a real Mac. No mockups, no sped-up fakes. If something is sped up, write "2x" on screen.
- **No "free forever".** Say "free" or "$0". The Terms allow paid features later.
- **Don't copy real reviews.** Write the hook in your own words.
- **Recording:** Cmd+Shift+5 → record a selected part of the screen, then crop to 9:16. Use a clean test account (no real names or emails on screen). Turn on Do Not Disturb so no notifications show up.

## The 10 videos

### 1. Paywalls (the biggest complaint)
- **Hook (0–2 s):** "Paying $10 a month… to block ONE app?"
- **Shots:** sign up → the receipt prints $0.00 and falls → open Blocked apps and add five apps.
- **End card:** "Locken. $0. No card."
- **Caption:** Blocking distractions shouldn't cost a subscription.

### 2. Bugs and freezes
- **Hook:** "Your focus app froze. Again."
- **Shots:** one unedited take: open Locken → type a goal → Start → open a game and watch it close. Timer visible the whole time.
- **End card:** "One take. No cuts."
- **Note:** don't say "bug-free". The one-take video is the proof.

### 3. "The update ruined it"
- **Hook:** "They redesigned your focus app and now it takes 6 taps to start."
- **Shots:** count on screen: 1, type the goal; 2, pick the time; 3, Start.
- **End card:** "3 steps. Then you're focusing."

### 4. Too easy to get around
- **Hook:** "Turned off your blocker in 2 seconds?"
- **Shots:** during a session press Cmd+Q → the 20-second "Stay with it?" countdown → press Keep focusing. Then Force Quit Locken → reopen it → the session picks up with the time left.
- **End card:** "Quitting is a choice, not a reflex."
- **Note:** be honest. It's a countdown, not a cage. Don't say "impossible to quit".

### 5. Confusing and cluttered
- **Hook:** "Needing a tutorial for a focus app is crazy."
- **Shots:** the whole start flow on one screen with no voiceover, then the menu bar timer counting down.
- **End card:** "Open it. Start it. Done."

### 6. Features taken away
- **Hook:** "They took away the timer?"
- **Shots:** pick 25, 50 or 90 minutes → the timer runs → zoom in on the menu bar lock and time left.
- **End card:** "Timed sessions. Right in your menu bar."

### 7. No Mac or desktop
- **Hook:** "Your blocker only works on your phone. Your homework is on your laptop."
- **Shots:** MacBook on a desk → start a session → open Steam or Discord and watch it close.
- **End card:** "Locken for Mac. Windows too."

### 8. Blocks the wrong things
- **Hook:** "Your blocker locked you out of your school app."
- **Shots:** during a session open Canvas, Google Docs and Notes: all fine. Open a game: closed.
- **End card:** "Blocks games, social and YouTube. Never your work."

### 9. Ads
- **Hook:** "Ads… inside a FOCUS app?"
- **Shots:** scroll slowly through the dashboard, Focus, Habits and Settings. No ads anywhere.
- **End card:** "Zero ads. Zero trackers."

### 10. Support never answers
- **Hook:** "Emailed support. Heard nothing. Ever."
- **Shots:** send feedback from inside Locken → cut to you reading it on the owner view → your reply.
- **End card:** "The person who made it reads every message."
- **Note:** post this only if you'll keep answering feedback.

## Bonus: what nobody else does
- **Hook:** "Your focus app has no idea what your homework is."
- **Shots:** Start session → Canvas assignments appear → pick one → the AI writes the steps → Start.
