Locken

Download Locken

The free focus app for Mac. Pick the assignment, start the timer, and Locken closes games, social media and YouTube until you're done.

Download for Mac

Requires macOS 15 Sequoia or later, on an Apple silicon or Intel Mac.

Installing

  1. Open Locken.dmg and drag Locken into Applications.
  2. Open Locken from your Applications folder.
  3. If macOS says it can't check Locken for malware, open System Settings, go to Privacy & Security, and click Open Anyway.
